Contributing to NDGI
Thanks for your interest in contributing. NDGI is a Unreal Engine 5 plugin that implements the CVPR 2026 paper Neural Dynamic GI (Wu et al.) — temporal lightmaps compressed into per-tile MLPs trained in compute shaders, with no external ML framework. The project is maintained by me, so small, focused contributions with measured results are the easiest to review and merge.


When reporting bugs please provide a way to reproduce the bugs
